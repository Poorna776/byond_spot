ABOUT BYOND SPOT

Travel beyond the usual. Discover what others miss.

BYOND Spot is an intelligent tourism platform designed to help travelers discover destinations, local services and experiences based on their interests, budget, time and surroundings.

Instead of directing everyone toward the same popular tourist attractions, BYOND Spot helps travelers discover suitable lesser-known places while creating better opportunities for local communities and helping reduce overcrowding.
